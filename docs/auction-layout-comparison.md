Design exploration for ticket 0, not production implementation. The user approved an interactive browser comparison within the light-parchment Middle-earth atlas identity, then selected **A — Council triptych (Divan)**. The complete approved prototype is recorded in [ODI Bid Battle — Design Record](./odi-bid-battle-design.md). Production stack, final fonts, final artwork, and production implementation remain open.

## Additional approved preparation layouts

- **Guided preparation:** Battlefield → Candidate List → Teams → Final Review. Show a step indicator and allow returning to previous steps. Save incomplete preparation as a draft; the final review presents initial-budget equality, candidate-count requirements, and other start blockers together.
- **Candidate list editor:** Searchable candidate catalog on the left, ordered selected candidate list on the right. Both panes scroll independently. Add, remove, and reorder candidates on the same screen. Provide move-up/move-down controls as an alternative to drag-and-drop. This composition applies to candidate-list editing, including the guided preparation step, while preserving the approved copy-on-edit rules.
- **Team preparation:** Two side-by-side panels. Edit member names, optional avatars, and positive integer initial gold directly in each row. Add members inline and move them to the other team using a button. Keep calculated team totals and their difference visible above the scrolling member lists.

## Research applied

UI/UX Pro Max's design-system search for `fantasy parchment auction desktop` returned a dark auction/operations direction. It conflicts with the pinned light-parchment brief and was rejected. A focused `historical parchment natural textures` style search returned Organic Biophilic and Nature Distilled: their natural color families and restrained surface detail are relevant, but their wellness positioning and rounded organic component recipes are not adopted. No exact verified atlas-auction style match was found. Composition below is product-specific design judgment.

Impeccable supplied the product-context workflow, Operate-mode priority on readable state, and surface composition seed `b9489a66`. The dealt structures are positions 4, 6, and 7 of the grounded list below. Catalog challengers did not replace the user-pinned world.

## Grounded composition candidates

1. Bound chronicle: narrow navigation margin and a single working page.
2. Cartographer's desk: candidate image above the two team registers.
3. Auction docket: side-by-side full registers with a small shared candidate header.
4. Council triptych: team registers flank a central candidate and sale controls.
5. Herald's board: dominant bid at the top and compressed supporting registers.
6. Opposing folios: full-width candidate banner above two wide team registers.
7. Campaign ledger: persistent candidate at left and stacked horizontal team registers at right.

## Comparison contracts

- **A — Council triptych:** central candidate is the anchor; mirrored team panels emphasize rivalry. Trade-off: member names receive less width.
- **B — Opposing folios:** candidate banner precedes two broad registers. Trade-off: less vertical space for the candidate image.
- **C — Campaign ledger:** left candidate and right stacked registers favor scanning member contributions. Trade-off: less symmetrical stage presence.

All variants use the same synthetic state, preserve full candidate artwork, and keep balances, latest confirmed bid, and actions outside member-list scrolling. The prototype demonstrates contribution editing, confirmation/cancellation, image aspect options, and reversible sample sales; it is not the complete auction engine.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Compare three readable live-auction compositions without changing the approved atlas identity.

OWN-WORLD: Light parchment, brown ink, forest-green controls and branch marks; ember red reserved for consequential states. Serif headings and clear tabular numbers.

STORY: Identify the candidate and confirmed bid, inspect members, and confirm or cancel a sale.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Three equal comparison controls precede one full-size live sample. A centers the candidate; B leads with a candidate banner; C anchors the candidate at left. Each places sale controls beside the candidate.

FORM: Grounded candidates 4, 6, and 7; surface seed b9489a66. The user selected candidate 4, Council triptych (A — Divan).

FINISH: This comparison ends with browser checks and user selection; complete application design, final artwork, and production implementation remain later work.
