# ODI Bid Battle

ODI Bid Battle is a two-team auction in which team members contribute gold to acquire candidates. An operator manages the auction and presents the resulting teams.

## Language

**Auction**:
The complete event, encompassing its preparation, bidding rounds, and final team presentation.
_Avoid_: Bidding round when referring to the entire event.

**Bidding Round**:
The bidding process for one candidate, ending in a sale or an allowed skip.
_Avoid_: Auction when referring only to one candidate.

**Candidate**:
A named, illustrated entity that teams can acquire through bidding, distinct from a team member.
_Avoid_: Team member, participant.

**Candidate List**:
An ordered, reusable collection of candidates available for an auction.
_Avoid_: Team roster, unordered candidate pool.

**Team**:
One of the two competing groups, identified by a name, flag, and slogan, whose members fund its bids.
_Avoid_: Bidder when the distinction between a team and its members matters.

**Team Member**:
A person belonging to a team who has an individual gold balance and contributes gold to its bids.
_Avoid_: Candidate, operator.

**Battlefield**:
The named setting selected for an auction, with an image, geographical description, and historical background.
_Avoid_: Bidding round, auction screen.

**Operator**:
The person controlling preparation, bids, sales, and presentation on behalf of both teams.
_Avoid_: Team member, bidder.

**Bid**:
A team's confirmed offer for the active candidate, equal to the sum of its members' confirmed contributions.
_Avoid_: Draft contribution, sale.

**Member Contribution**:
The amount of gold a team member offers toward the team's bid for a particular candidate.
_Avoid_: Initial gold, remaining balance.

**Sale**:
The confirmed award of a candidate to the team holding the latest confirmed bid, at that bid's price.
_Avoid_: Bid, skip.

**Team Capacity**:
The maximum number of candidates a team may acquire, equal to half the auction's initial candidate count.
_Avoid_: Gold budget, remaining gold.

**Scheduled Starting Team**:
The team designated to open a round by the candidate's position in the auction list, before eligibility is considered.
_Avoid_: Last bidding team, actual opening team.

**Eligible Team**:
A team with at least one gold remaining and room below its team capacity.
_Avoid_: Team able to outbid, since eligibility alone does not guarantee enough gold to exceed an existing bid.

**Skipped Candidate**:
A candidate left unassigned after the permitted opening pass, who is not offered again in the same auction.
_Avoid_: Unsold candidate when including candidates that were never presented.

**Archived Candidate**:
A candidate hidden from the catalog but retained in the candidate lists and auctions that reference it.
_Avoid_: Deleted candidate when implying permanent removal.
